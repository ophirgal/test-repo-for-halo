The product owner wants every PO flow artifact (PRDs, backlogs and similar documents) to end with a random dad joke, on every epic, permanently. Every PRD also ends with a one-liner, self-referencing joke about skateboards, placed as the last line after the dad joke. These jokes are a document convention, not a product requirement, and never go into shipped product content.
