Choices made in this prototype

Direction

  • Structured and calm: a warm light palette, rounded cards and an app bar on every screen.
  • Welcome explains the game in three short steps under the art.
  • The score sits in a chip above the board. Pause is a full-width button in the bottom bar, not a corner icon.
  • Paused is a bottom sheet over the faded board. Game over puts the final score in a card first, with the crash snapshot under it.

Taken from the structure

  • All four screens are new: no game exists in the product today.
  • Controls: swipe anywhere to steer, with no arrow pad (open branch 1, recommended option).
  • Score: this round's score only, with no best score saved (open branch 2).
  • Pause: a Pause button, plus an automatic pause when the app leaves the screen (open branch 3).
  • Speed: rises a little as the score grows (open branch 4). It cannot be seen in a still page.

Prototype only

  • With no script, the board doesn't move. Tap the board on 02 Game — playing to jump to the crash on 03 Game over.
  • “Road Snake” is a sample game name. Swap it freely.
  • Sample round: score 12 while playing and paused, final score 14 after hitting the right wall.
  • The food pulse and the tongue flick are CSS animations, and both stop when the phone asks for reduced motion.
