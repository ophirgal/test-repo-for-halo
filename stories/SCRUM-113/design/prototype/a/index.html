Screens shown at phone size, 390 × 844 portrait, as the story targets mobile phones. Solid arrows move forward; dashed arrows go back.

Choices made in this prototype

Direction

  • Dark, full-screen and immersive, kind to eyes in a car at night.
  • The snake glows (cyan to lime gradient, patterned back, eyes and tongue).
  • Pause is a small round button in the top corner; Paused is a centred overlay with one big Resume button.
  • Big type: the final score is the hero of Game over.

From the approved structure

  • Steering: swipe anywhere on the play field (structure default). No on-screen arrows.
  • Walls: hitting the edge or the snake’s own tail ends the game.
  • Score: one point per berry; Game over shows this run’s score only, no best score.
  • Pause: a paused state of the Game screen, also opened when the app goes to the background.
  • Portrait only, no sound.

Prototype only

  • Pages are still frames, so the snake does not move. Tap the play field on Game to jump to the crash and see Game over.
  • The berry pulses gently (stops when the phone asks for reduced motion).
  • Sample run: score 14, snake 15 squares long.
  • Game name used on screen: “Snake”.

Next step

  • The chosen concept’s screens drawn in full; the three screens have no loading, empty or error states.
