Choices made in this set

  • The open-branch defaults from the structure apply. Validation runs when the visitor presses Subscribe. If both fields are wrong, both messages show (drawn separately here, as the inventory names them). After a failure, pressing Subscribe again is the retry. Back to the site goes to the site home, which this gallery stands in for.
  • Each error message sits right under its control, in words, with an icon and a red border. The field is marked aria-invalid and linked to its message with aria-describedby.
  • Loading: Subscribe is disabled, greyed and shows a spinner with "Subscribing…". The visitor's input is read-only meanwhile. The review bar lets you simulate the answer: success leads to Confirmation, failure to Submission failed.
  • Submission failed: an alert above the form says the sign-up didn't go through and why, and the email address and the ticked consent are kept.
  • Confirmation is identical for new and returning visitors. Nothing says whether the address was already on the list.
  • The mobile pages are separate files drawn at a 390px phone width, with no media queries. Controls are at least 48px tall and nothing scrolls sideways.
  • The clicks follow the diagram. Subscribe on Default (empty field) leads to Invalid email; on Invalid email it leads to Consent missing; on Consent missing it leads to Loading.
