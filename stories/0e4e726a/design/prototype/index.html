Newsletter sign-up: clickable prototype

This prototype walks the main path of the approved structure. A visitor reads what the newsletter is on Welcome, gives an email address and ticks the consent on the Sign-up form, and lands on Confirmation, which tells them to check their inbox. No account is needed. Every page is built for phone and desktop: resize the window to check that nothing scrolls sideways.

Start the journey

Screens

  1. Welcome: what the newsletter is, and Sign up
  2. Sign-up form: email address, consent, Subscribe, Back
  3. Confirmation: check your inbox, the address the mail went to, Back to the site

Choices made in this prototype

  • Only the main path is drawn. The empty Welcome, the invalid-email, consent-missing, loading and submission-failed states are covered in the Design step.
  • Without scripts, Subscribe is a link that goes straight to Confirmation, so you can type anything. The loading state between the two is not shown here.
  • The consent checkbox is real and starts unticked. Tick it as a visitor would.
  • A three-step indicator at the top of each screen shows where the visitor is.
  • Back to the site on Confirmation goes to this page, which stands in for the public site's home page (default from open branch 5).
