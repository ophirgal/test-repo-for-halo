Newsletter signup journey — prototype

This prototype covers the main path of SCRUM-102. A visitor follows the newsletter link in the site footer, reads the one-sentence welcome, enters a name and email address, and sees the confirmation. The visitor's details are filled in on every page (Maya Levi, maya.levi@example.com). Each page has a way back. Narrow the viewer to under 480 px to see the mobile layout. The error, submitting and submission-failed states of the form are drawn in the next step.

Choices made in this prototype

  • Matcha theme, as you asked. Every colour is now a shade of matcha green: a pale matcha page (#eef4e2), matcha buttons, links and progress bar (#4a6b25, darker #3a541c on hover), a deep matcha focus ring (#2d4214), and soft matcha borders. Every pairing meets WCAG 2.1 AA: white on the buttons is 6.1:1, body text 13.4:1, the focus ring 11:1, and input borders 4.2:1. Layout, type and spacing still come from the approved newsletter design in story 0e4e726a, as the structure recommended.
  • A matcha illustration (a bowl of whisked matcha, a bamboo whisk, a tea scoop and leaves) tops the card on Welcome, Signup form and Confirmation. It is inline SVG, decorative and hidden from screen readers, so focus order is unchanged. It carries over the matcha image you asked for on the screens; it can be dropped without changing anything else.
  • On the site page, only the new footer takes the matcha theme. The rest of the page stays as it is today, because restyling the existing site is outside this story.
  • The Name field is capped at 80 characters (maxlength), as recommended in the structure. There is no "name too long" error.
  • A three-step progress bar (Welcome, Your details, Done) shows the visitor's current step.
  • The confirmation does not mention a confirmation email, because this epic has no double opt-in.
  • The design viewer runs no scripts, so "Submit" is a link straight to the confirmation.
