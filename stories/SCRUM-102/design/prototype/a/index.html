Choices made in this prototype
  • Direction — Focused: each page is one narrow centred card (560 px) with a three-step progress bar above it. Nothing competes with the one thing to do on the page; on a phone the card simply fills the width.
  • Visual base: the new screens reuse the tokens of the approved newsletter design in story 0e4e726a, as the structure decided. Two changes for WCAG 2.1 AA: the focus ring is dark blue (#1f3cae) instead of yellow, and input borders are darker (#8a93a3), so both reach 3:1.
  • Desktop and mobile: every page has one layout that adapts to a phone width. The thumbnails show each page at 1280×800 with its 390×844 phone view in front.
  • On the site page, the only change is a new footer with a “Newsletter” link; the page’s content is centred and otherwise as it is today. The footer also links back to this journey, for review only.
  • The Name field is capped at 80 characters (maxlength), as the structure decided; there is no “name too long” error.
  • A three-step progress indicator (Welcome, Your details, Done) shows the visitor’s current step.
  • The confirmation does not mention a confirmation email: this epic has no double opt-in. New and already-subscribed addresses see the same page.
  • The design viewer runs no scripts, so “Submit” is a link straight to the confirmation. Each screen also has a “← Back” link to the previous one.
  • The error, submitting and submission-failed states of the form are drawn in the next step.
