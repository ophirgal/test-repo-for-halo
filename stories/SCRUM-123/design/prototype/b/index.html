Each screen is shown as a desktop browser at 1280×800 with a phone at 390×844 over its corner, at the same scale, because the story asks for desktop and mobile widths.

Choices made in this prototype
  • Signpost: the content is centred in the window both ways. A large grey “404” leads, and the link is an outlined pill, at least 48 px tall, that fills blue on hover. It is still a link (it goes somewhere), not a button.
  • Copy as decided in the structure: headline “Page not found”, the sentence “The link you followed may be broken, or the page may have moved.”, and one link, “Go to the home page”.
  • The broken URL is not shown back to the visitor (structure, open branch 1). There is no search or analytics, and no link to /jungle.
  • The “404” mark is plain text in the page font. It is hidden from screen readers, so the headline is the first thing they announce.
  • Look taken from docs/index.html: white background, system-ui, 1rem padding, the browser’s default black text and blue link (9.4:1 contrast). The link has a 3 px black focus outline for keyboard users.
  • In the prototype the link goes to home.html, which stands in for /. Home keeps today’s content. It is centred and has a small prototype-only link back to step 01, so you are never stuck there.
  • “Browser Back” from the structure leads off the site, so it is not drawn. The page has no states other than the default, so the next step draws this screen at both widths.
