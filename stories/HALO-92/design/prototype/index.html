Jungle page "Hello World!": clickable prototype

This prototype shows the one new screen in the approved structure: the static page at /jungle. It has a large yellow "Hello World!" on a deep-green background, framed by original plant shapes. These are leaf clusters in the top corners, vines on the sides, and bushes with small flowers along an earth-brown ground. The product owner reviews it in two widths, desktop and phone at 375 px, and approves or rejects each one. Each screen page shows the design inside a frame, with the exact colours and the rule for what shrinks or hides at 375 px beside it.

Choices made in this prototype

  • The open-branch defaults from the structure are applied as drawn. Desktop is 1440 px, with no separate tablet layout. At 375 px the heading wraps to "Hello" / "World!". The corner leaves and bushes shrink and the side vines are hidden. The heading is yellow on deep green. The font is the system bold font.
  • The jungle page has no links or buttons (AC 4). The bar above each frame and the notes below it belong to the prototype, not to the page.
  • The page is static: no motion, no sound (R13). All decorations are inline shapes drawn for this page. There are no images, external fonts or animals (R9, AC 4).
  • The page scales to the window. Make the window narrow on the desktop view and it switches to the 375 px layout, the same way the built page will behave.
  • Approval and rejection with feedback (AC 5) happen in the cockpit's review, not on these pages.
