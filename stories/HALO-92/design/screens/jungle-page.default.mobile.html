Build notes for HALO-93

  • Tab title: Hello World! · Jungle, which includes "Hello World" (R8). lang="en".
  • One <h1>, "Hello World!", is the only text on the page. No links, buttons, forms or other text (AC 4).
  • Decorations are inline SVG shapes drawn for this page, marked aria-hidden="true". There are no image files, web fonts, animals or background photo (R9, AC 4).
  • System bold font only. No motion, transitions or sound (R13).
  • Everything ships in the page and one stylesheet. Nothing is loaded from outside the site, and there is no tracking (R10, R11).
  • The safe zone is the centre of the page, clear of every decoration (R5). Decorations sit below the heading in stacking order (z-index 1 vs 2).
