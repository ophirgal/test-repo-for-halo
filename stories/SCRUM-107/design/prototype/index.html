Corner Bean — home screen prototype

This prototype walks the main path of SCRUM-107, a clone of SCRUM-106 (itself cloned from SCRUM-105): a neighbourhood customer opens the cafe's mobile app and lands on Home, where they see which cafe this is, whether it is open right now and where it is, today's favourites, and one-tap entry points to the rest of the app. As the approved structure decided, Home is rebuilt from the approved SCRUM-106 design with the same layout, labels, tokens and sample content.

Choices carried over

  • Content: brand header, "Open now" status with today's hours, address with Directions, "Today's favourites" and "View full menu". No ordering.
  • Navigation: bottom tab bar (Home · Menu · Visit · More) with Home selected.
  • Anonymous: no sign-in, greeting or loyalty card.
  • Leaving the story: menu, item detail, directions and the other tabs are out of scope. Tapping one opens an "out of scope" note with a way back to Home instead of a page.
  • Loading, error and empty states: drawn in the next step. Pull to refresh appears as a hint at the top of Home.
